
Hello ADVANCE Instructor. This guide will walk you through the process of creating your syllabus using the syllabus template prepared by Gateway Seminary faculty. This guide incorporates each element of the syllabus template, plus instructions and advice specific to you as the instructor. The guide will coach you through making edits directly to the syllabus template in order to produce your student-ready syllabus. At the end of this guide, your student-ready syllabus will be output as a separate file for you to share with students and automatically submitted for approval by the ADVANCE office. Depending on the kinds of changes you made, your syllabus may be approved immediately.
